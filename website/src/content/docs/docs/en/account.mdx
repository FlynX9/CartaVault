## Open the panel

Open the menu attached to your avatar, then choose **Options**. The user panel contains settings for your own account and remains separate from instance-wide administration.

## Available sections

- **Profile**: display name, avatar, and account information.
- **Preferences**: language, theme, density, start screen, time zone, and trash retention.
- **Services and API keys**: search, routing, classic basemap, and satellite providers using personal or quota-shared instance keys.
- **Security**: email, password, TOTP or email MFA, recovery codes, and active sessions.
- **Privacy**: personal export and data-processing information.
- **Offline data**: maps and trips stored in this browser.

Instance keys are selectable when an administrator shares them with your quota profile. Their secrets remain hidden and users cannot edit them.

## Save changes safely

The **Save** button applies panel changes. When closing with unsaved changes, CartaVault offers to continue editing, discard the changes, or save them.
