## A touch-first interface

On mobile, CartaVault keeps the map at the centre of the experience and adapts panels to the available screen height. The bottom navigation gives access to maps, places, trips, media, and organisation. Cards and dialogs open between the top bar and bottom navigation so they remain easy to close and browse.

<ProductScreenshot src={places} alt="CartaVault places list on a phone" caption="The places list adapts to a phone screen with direct touch navigation." />

## Places and map

From **Places**, browse the list, search for a POI, and open its record. **Show on map** switches to the map centred on that place; the Places panel then becomes a compact strip. Tap the strip to return to the list.

Secondary actions are available by swiping place cards, keeping small screens free of permanently visible controls.

## Trips and timeline

Days, nights, departure and arrival are shown as cards. Use the trip's lower navigation, arrows, or a swipe to move through the itinerary. Trip and day options open in dialogs that adapt to the available height.

Tapping **Trips** again opens the **Timeline**. The timeline lets you browse stops and highlights the selected stop on the map without an unwanted zoom.

## Tips

- Scroll long lists vertically; filters are collapsed by default.
- Close a dialog with its close button or by moving to another section with the bottom navigation.
- Use swipe actions only on the intended areas: stop cards keep their own actions.
