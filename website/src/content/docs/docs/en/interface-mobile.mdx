## A touch-first interface

On mobile, CartaVault keeps the map at the centre of the experience and adapts panels to the available screen height. The bottom navigation remains global with **Home**, **Maps**, **Media**, and **Trash**. When a map is open, a scrollable context bar below the header identifies it and provides **Places**, **Trips**, **Organisation**, and permitted map actions. Cards and dialogs open between these bars so they remain easy to close and browse.

## Places and map

From **Places**, browse the list, search for a POI, and open its record. **Show on map** switches to the map centred on that place; the Places panel then becomes a compact strip. Tap the strip to return to the list.

Secondary actions are available by swiping place cards, keeping small screens free of permanently visible controls.

## Trips and timeline

Days, nights, departure and arrival are shown as cards. Use the trip's lower navigation, arrows, or a swipe to move through the itinerary. Trip and day options open in dialogs that adapt to the available height.

The **Timeline** mode remains available from the Trips panel. It lets you browse stops and highlights the selected stop on the map without an unwanted zoom.

## Tips

- Scroll long lists vertically; filters are collapsed by default.
- Close a dialog with its close button or move to another section with the map context bar.
- Use swipe actions only on the intended areas: stop cards keep their own actions.
