## Access model

Permissions are assigned per map. Having an account or being an instance administrator is not enough to open another user's private map. The server enforces every sensitive operation, including manually entered URLs.

## Invite a member

1. Open **Maps**.
2. Choose **Members** on the target map.
3. Enter the recipient's email address.
4. Select **Viewer** or **Editor**.
5. Send the invitation.

An existing user receives an in-app notification and, when email is configured, a transactional message. A recipient without an account receives a link to create one before accepting access.

## Respond to an invitation

Pending invitations appear in the notification center and map catalog. The recipient can accept or decline. The map joins their workspace only after acceptance.

## Change or remove access

The owner can switch a member between viewer and editor, or remove access. Removal applies to subsequent requests and does not transfer or delete map data.

## Transfer ownership

Ownership transfer is a proposal, not an immediate mutation:

1. the owner enters the future owner's address;
2. CartaVault sends a transfer invitation;
3. the recipient explicitly accepts;
4. ownership changes and the previous owner keeps the role defined by the server.

If the address has no account, the recipient is invited to register and then claim ownership. Do not delete your account before the transfer is accepted.

## Revoke an invitation

An invitation can be revoked while pending. A revoked, expired, or already consumed link no longer grants access.

## Recommended practice

- grant viewer access by default;
- reserve editor access for people who need to change data;
- review members before exports or ownership transfers;
- transfer ownership instead of sharing account credentials;
- promptly remove access that is no longer required.
