## Add a place

Use the **+** button in the Places panel, search for an address on the map, or import a KML/KMZ file. At minimum, provide a name and location. Visit duration defaults to 30 minutes when no value is set.

## Complete the record

A place can include a description, category, status, tags, a half-star rating, photos, and multiple named links. Images can also be pasted from the clipboard when editing is allowed.

<ProductScreenshot src={popup} alt="Detailed CartaVault place record" caption="The record keeps useful information together without overloading the map." />

The map media library then provides access to photos attached to places. Night-accommodation images remain separate from place media.

<ProductScreenshot src={media} alt="CartaVault map media library" caption="Media remains linked to its source place." />

## Find a place

Combine text search, facets, and sorting. **Reset filters** clears active filters without changing your persisted sorting preference.

## Share a map

The owner can invite a viewer or editor, then transfer ownership through email confirmation. Administrators do not automatically gain access to private user maps.
